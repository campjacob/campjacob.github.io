--- layout: foundation nav_group: blog title: "Snapshots: My Blog" excerpt: "Little bits and pieces of my life written down and shared with you." --- {%- comment -%} The blog's front page. jekyll-paginate makes the pages (/blog/, /blog/page2/, ...; paginate and paginate_path in _config.yml), but the posts on each are picked here: page 1 is the newest post as a large card plus the next 8 in a grid, and every later page is 8 more, so each grid comes out even. (paginator.posts would put the newest post's slot back on page 2 and leave an odd card at the bottom.) The page numbers sit on either side of Newer and Older: the first and last pages and the two either side of this one, with … for the rest. Each is titled with the dates it covers, to skip between years.
