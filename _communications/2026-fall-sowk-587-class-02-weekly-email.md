## Unit Introduction and What You Will Learn

Week two is asynchronous. Students will read about a typical day for a school social worker and some content published by the School Social Work Association of America. You will read and discuss the common tasks, roles, and skills required of a school-based social worker. The following are some of the week's learning objectives.

- Describe key responsibilities and daily activities of school social workers.
- Apply theoretical frameworks to real-world student situations.
- Reflect on personal and professional competencies needed for effective school-based practice.
- Identify and analyze essential skills used by school social workers in diverse educational settings.
