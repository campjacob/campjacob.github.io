## Unit Resources

Knowing that school is stressful, i feel like a valuable resource to share this week is three of the distress tolerance skills that DBT teaches.

- Radical Acceptance
- Self-Soothe with Senses
- Distraction (ACCEPTS)

[The Therapist Aid Worksheet](https://www.therapistaid.com/therapy-worksheet/dbt-distress-tolerance-skills) provides a good overview of what these are and how to implement them.
