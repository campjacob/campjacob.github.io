## Unit Introduction and What You Will Learn

There is a wide range of school-related problems that social workers can assist in addressing through evidence-based practices and community, human, and technological resources. Week nine, an asynchronous week, is designed to help students gain a more well-defined understanding of the problems and awareness of resources that can help address them. Students will read chapter eight of Jarolmen and Bautista-Thomas (2023), which reviews issues critical for school social workers. Students engage in forums to share their experiences with problems and explore a broad range of resources. The lecture video for students this week includes practical content on saving resources to share with clients and colleagues, as well as information about the fact sheet assignment.

The agenda for the lecture video is as follows:

- Content for week nine
- Saving informational resources
- School-related problem fact sheet

The learning objectives for week nine include:

- Identify and define school-related problems social workers commonly encounter.
- Demonstrate an understanding of various resources (human, community, and technological) that can be leveraged to address school-related challenges.
- Evaluate strategies for effectively integrating community resources into school settings to provide wraparound services, particularly in rural and small district contexts.
- Demonstrate practical skills in collecting, organizing, and sharing informational resources valuable for professional social work practice in schools.

**W-09 A-01: Asynchronous Participation and Engagement**

The expectation is that each of your replies will be substantive and provide meaningful perspectives, contributing to the forum's conversation and scholarship. They can be related to the prompts or build on conversations shared by peers. There are _five forums_ for this week, and you are expected to make at least **6 replies** across any of the forums and read all of your peers' replies. These forums include the following:

- Students begin developing ideas for their school-related problem factsheet in [Defining a Problem and Preparing for School-Related Problem Factsheet](https://myheritage.heritage.edu/ICS/Academics/SOWK/SOWK_587/2627_FA-SOWK_587-0/💻_W-09_1019-1025.jnz?portlet=Group_Discussion_Forums&screen=PostView&screenType=change&id=5fece6ea-1d96-47aa-a8c1-09f125aaa55b), sharing topics they are considering and what draws them to those issues.
- [Community Resources Sharing](https://myheritage.heritage.edu/ICS/Academics/SOWK/SOWK_587/2627_FA-SOWK_587-0/💻_W-09_1019-1025.jnz?portlet=Group_Discussion_Forums&screen=PostView&screenType=change&id=04921022-932f-4d11-aa06-d1ec7bce1aba) asks students to identify and share actionable community resources relevant to their factsheet topics, with particular attention to the unique challenges of rural service settings.
- Human, community, and technological resources in school settings are the focus of [Technological and Human Resources](https://myheritage.heritage.edu/ICS/Academics/SOWK/SOWK_587/2627_FA-SOWK_587-0/💻_W-09_1019-1025.jnz?portlet=Group_Discussion_Forums&screen=PostView&screenType=change&id=e50da4ac-828e-4871-a784-d85651494c37)where students explore collaboration, universal design, and tools that support student equity and access.
- Students discuss how the resources and tools used in school social work practice connect to culturally responsive service delivery in [Intersection of Supportive Resources and Culturally Responsive Practices](https://myheritage.heritage.edu/ICS/Academics/SOWK/SOWK_587/2627_FA-SOWK_587-0/💻_W-09_1019-1025.jnz?portlet=Group_Discussion_Forums&screen=PostView&screenType=change&id=994bb612-3cfc-455f-b3bc-932a30509b59).
- [Chapter Eight Textbook Questions](https://myheritage.heritage.edu/ICS/Academics/SOWK/SOWK_587/2627_FA-SOWK_587-0/💻_W-09_1019-1025.jnz?portlet=Group_Discussion_Forums&screen=PostView&screenType=change&id=1e254a54-f618-4308-a8a5-ddca28866ba4) invites students to respond to prompts from Jarolmen and Bautista-Thomas (2023) on state policy needs, serving diverse student populations, and the school social worker's role in policy development.
