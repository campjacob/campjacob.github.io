## Unit Introduction and What You Will Learn

We start moving away from our focus on the standards in week ten. This week is a primer on what you will be diving into over the next several weeks. Students will listen to a Social Work Podcast episode and begin learning the steps of ethical problem-solving. There are three case studies that students can practice implementing these steps. Students will also complete the code of ethics exam. During the lecture video the following is the agenda:

- Course content discussion (midcourse feedback and the A-03 exam)
- Introduction to the Essential Steps for Ethical Problem-Solving
- Practice applying the steps with case studies in the forums

**Week 10 Objectives**

- Demonstrate a working knowledge of the _Code of Ethics_ through earning a satisfactory score on the code exam.
- Begin to utilize the Essential Steps method for addressing ethical issues.
- Utilize case studies to demonstrate critical thinking in complex circumstances.

**A-03: NASW Code of Ethics Standards and Principles Exam**

**Meta**: _Points_ 100 points (20% of total points); _Deadline_ Monday 11/02/26 at 08:00 AM; _Completion_ via MyHeritage Assignments as an online test _A-03: NASW Code of Ethics Standards and Principles Exam_ ([Sec 0](https://myheritage.heritage.edu/ICS/Academics/SOWK/SOWK_430/2627_FA-SOWK_430-0/Assignments.jnz?portlet=Coursework&screen=AssignmentDetailView&screenType=change&id=27629ada-a6d6-4d8c-bd0c-efd17daa8160) or [Sec 2](https://myheritage.heritage.edu/ICS/Academics/SOWK/SOWK_430/2627_FA-SOWK_430-2/Assignments.jnz?portlet=Coursework&screen=AssignmentDetailView&screenType=change&id=b8673c98-e561-472c-ab1a-a8e082309ce1))

**Purpose**: The _NASW Code of Ethics Standards and Principles Exam_ measures students' understanding of the 2021 NASW Code of Ethics and its applications.

**Task**: Students will work to complete an online exam regarding the NASW Code of Ethics and its standards and principles. The test is open-book, and students can use any notes they might have. The exam includes 20 prompts, including multiple-choice and true/false questions. Each question is worth five points.

**Success**: Students will correctly answer the questions in the exam. The results for the questions should be available after the test due date has passed. Students may take as much time as they need until the deadline. If the test is submitted prematurely, it can be reopened.

## Unit Resources

The [NASW Code of Ethics](https://www.socialworkers.org/About/Ethics/Code-of-Ethics/Code-of-Ethics-English) is available online and in your textbook and will be valuable to have nearby when you are doing your exam this week.

You can read the [Essential Steps for Ethical Decision Making](https://www.naswma.org/page/100/Essential-Steps-for-Ethical-Problem-Solving.htm) on the National Association of Social Workers - Massachusetts Chapter (n.d.) website or the sale NASW Press page for Reamer and Conrad (1995).

The three case studies for this week's forums are handouts here: _Case Study Practice: Terminating Services_, _Case Study Practice: Confidentiality and Protecting Third Parties_, and _Case Study Practice: End of Life Issues_. Each one has the case and Reamer's commentary from Appendix B of your textbook, Reamer (2023), along with the standards to start from and the four steps to work through.

Go to [The Journal of Social Work Values and Ethics](http://jswve.org/current-issue/) website and sign up to receive a free e-copy of the journal that comes out twice per year. It can be great way to stay current in the field. Volume two focuses a lot on social justice and anti-racist engagement. One article that I found interesting was  Disney and Carnes (2022) who provide a number of specific ethical dilemmas and consider how to best respond. Feel free to browse any of the articles listed there.

All of the presentations for this class can be found at <https://presentations.jacobrcampbell.com>. This week's slides are [Fall 2026 SOWK 430 Week 10 - Introduction To Essential Steps for Ethical Problem-Solving](https://presentations.jacobrcampbell.com/Ay1Q6C).
