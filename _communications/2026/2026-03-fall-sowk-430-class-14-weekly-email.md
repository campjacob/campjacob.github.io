## Unit Introduction and What You Will Learn

Week 14 is meant to be light, allowing students to enjoy Thanksgiving break and prepare for the end of class.

- Apply ethical boundaries to small community settings.
- Identify an area of boundaries in small communities that may affect your practice.
- Submit the final case study - the key assignment for the class
