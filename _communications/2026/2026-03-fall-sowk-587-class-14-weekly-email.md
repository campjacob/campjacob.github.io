## Unit Introduction and What You Will Learn

Week 14 is asynchronous. The reading provides many case studies and practice examples. The forums focus on sharing personal experiences with clients, reflecting on case studies, and engaging with the textbook content. There is no lecture video this week. 

**Learning Objectives**

- Demonstrate the ability to critically reflect on strategies, challenges, and interventions used in practice
- Apply course concepts to real-world scenarios by sharing and analyzing case examples

Chapter 11 ends with a short section on the emotional toll this work takes on social workers and interns. Jarolmen and Bautista-Thomas (2023) describe burnout as emotional exhaustion, depersonalization or cynicism, and diminished personal accomplishment, and they note that journaling helped social work interns relieve some of that toll (Moore et al., 2011, as cited in Jarolmen & Bautista-Thomas, 2023). Many of this week's vignettes are heavy, and you are likely carrying emotional toll from some of your own from your practicum.

The National Child Traumatic Stress Network (NCTSN) has a page on [Secondary Traumatic Stress](https://www.nctsn.org/trauma-informed-care/secondary-traumatic-stress), the emotional duress that comes from hearing about another person's firsthand trauma. They describe it as a common occupational hazard for professionals working with children who have experienced trauma, and they emphasize that both individual and supervisory awareness are part of protecting the worker and the children we serve (NCTSN, n.d.). That second point connects to this week's forum questions about using supervision well.

With Thanksgiving this week, I invite you to use part of the break for some self-reflection. What has this semester in your school asked of you? What are you grateful for in your practicum, whether a supervisor, a student who let you in, or a moment when an intervention worked? Gratitude doesn't erase the hard parts of this work, but taking stock of what sustains you alongside what drains you is part of self-care. You might even try the journaling the chapter mentions. Have a restful Thanksgiving.
