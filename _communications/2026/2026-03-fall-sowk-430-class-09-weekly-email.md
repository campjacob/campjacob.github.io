## Unit Introduction and What You Will Learn

Week nine is our last week focusing on the different statutes listed in the NASW code of ethics. These statutes include responsibilities that social workers have to our profession as social workers and to society itself.

This week we are covering:

- Ethical Standards 5 and 6
- Values: The Importance of Human Relationships

**Week 9 Objectives**:

- Understand and apply NASW's ethical responsibilities to the social work profession, including integrity of the profession and ethical evaluation and research.
- Understand and apply NASW's ethical responsibilities to the broader society, including social welfare, public participation, public emergencies, and social and political action.
- Explain how protections for research participants, such as informed consent and the right to withdraw, developed and why they matter in social work evaluation and research.
- Apply the principle of the importance of human relationships to personal experience.
