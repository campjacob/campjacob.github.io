## Unit Introduction and What You Will Learn

Week nine is asynchronous, with no in-person class. This week is dedicated to the _Teach Back Activity_. Each group recorded a video teaching their classmates how to assess one area of functioning from Hepworth et al. (2023) chapter nine, and each group has its own forum this week. Students watch the videos, engage with each group's material, and respond to questions about their own group's topic.

Learning objectives for the week include:

- Explain how to assess an area of client functioning to peers, including the questions a social worker might ask
- Apply assessment concepts from other groups' presentations to practice settings and client situations
- Engage in discussion that deepens understanding of biophysical, cognitive/perceptual, affective, behavioral, and environmental assessment

**W-09 A-01: Class Participation and Engagement**

The expectation is that each of your replies will be substantive and provide meaningful perspectives, contributing to the forum's conversation and scholarship. There are _five forums_ for this week, one for each teach back group. You are expected to make at least **3 replies** across the other groups' forums and to watch all of the videos. In your replies, you might:

- Share how you could apply the information in a practice setting or with a client
- Build on ideas the group brought up, or add ideas of your own
- Ask questions about the content
