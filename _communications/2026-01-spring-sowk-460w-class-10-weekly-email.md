## Unit Introduction and What You Will Learn

Week 10 is asynchronous. The topic is cost analysis for program evaluation. While none of your program evaluations include this, it is a helpful framework for students to learn about and understand. Royse (2023) describes cost-effectiveness and cost-benefit studies. In the forums, students can engage with the reading content, consider how they would design a study, and explore further literature.
